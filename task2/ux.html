Redesigned Product Page
by Iheagwara Augustine

Inspiration & Overview

This redesign was inspired by the IKEA product page (see inspiration link below), chosen for its effective clarity, clean structure, and conversion-focused layout. The goal was to modernize and streamline our product experience.

Inspiration: IKEA Product Page
Original Mockup: Visily Mockups

Problems Identified

  • Low Add-to-Cart conversion
  • Frequent customer support questions about estimated delivery time
  • Desire for a visual refresh that stays familiar

How the Redesign Solves Each Issue

Low Add-to-Cart Conversion
  • Large, high-contrast Add to Cart and Buy Now buttons.
  • Quantity selector beside CTAs for less friction and faster checkout.
  • Trust badges, ratings, and reviews next to price/CTA increase confidence.
  • Price hierarchy (sale price highlighted, list price de-emphasized).
Delivery Time Clarity
  • Delivery estimator placed under price/CTA, visible at decision point.
  • Real-time date or date-range estimate for entered postal code, fallback message if needed.
  • Conservative auto-estimate always visible for reassurance.
Modern Visuals, Familiar Flow
  • Fresh accent color, controlled whitespace, info cards, soft shapes.
  • Gallery → Title → Price → Actions order is preserved for returning users.
  • Feature icons/tiles let users scan for pros quickly; accordions/tabs keep content neat.

Developer Checklist & Implementation Ease

  • Components: modular, reusable (ProductGallery, PurchaseCard, DeliveryEstimator).
  • DeliveryEstimator connects easily to an API endpoint (GET /api/shipping/estimate).
  • All microcopy in English and Hungarian prepared up front for internationalization.
  • Design easily mapped to framework components (React, Vue, Angular).
  • Accessibility: ARIA labels, color contrast, and focus styles included.
  • Error and edge-case handling specified for all interactive areas.
